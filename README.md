# Week 2

## Project Brief & Brainstorming

This week we received our assigned project brief and groups. We began brainstorming about what the project might look like, through our eyes, and what sort of questions we have for our supervising professor.

The brief was short, but interesting, with lots of room for imagination and creativity. We are unsure about a lot of technical aspects of the project, but we still had some good ideas going about how we will approach this challenge. We each have a different skillset but the fusion of our expertise will work well; Jessica and I will be focusing more on technical development, and Leah and Sabrina will be spearheading design and user experience.

Some of our questions included the following:

- Is there 1 master controller for all the phones?
- What kind of control do you want from the phone side, if any?
- Are the phones or audience participatory?
- What is the upper limit of phones that can participate?
- Is each phone a single spacialisation point, or will they be grouped?
- How will we receive the audio?
- Is there a budget for a server?

From our understanding, the goal of this project is to simply create an immersive listening experience distributed across participants mobile phones. In collaboration with professor Gabriel Vigliensoni, we will develop this system as a plug and play tool to be used by the students of his CART 346 Digital sound class. 

In essence, our system will enable his students to present rich, spatialized soundscapes, that can be set up in any room or configuration using a set of mobile phones as speakers.

On the phone, a user will load our web app, then place the phone on the floor or a table. Over the following minutes, the phones will play back a sound piece distributed across space, through he mobile phone speakers. The composers/designers determine the sound sources, their timing, position of the phones, and the grouping of phones.
